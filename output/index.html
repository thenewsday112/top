Honda's Wildest Civic Type R Yet Is Being Developed for America

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

America Is Getting the Hardcore Civic Type R

Ever since the Honda Civic Type R HRC Concept appeared at the Tokyo Auto Salon earlier this year, we’ve heard plenty of reports about its future. There have been indications that the hardcore hot hatch is headed for production, along with rumors that it would eventually make its way to the US. However, Honda had never officially confirmed the latter until now.

In a release ahead of this year’s SEMA Show, Honda has confirmed that the Civic Type R HRC Concept is making its North American debut in Las Vegas from November 3 to 6. More importantly, Honda explicitly stated that the model is being developed for the US market.

Sure, this doesn’t mean you’ll be able to stroll into a dealership and drive one home tomorrow (at least not yet), but it’s a big leap from the endless speculation we’ve been hearing. The concept, which first broke cover in Japan, packs in HRC tech and feedback from real racing drivers to push the already sharp Civic Type R even further.

Honda

What to Expect From the Civic Type R HRC

Honda is still keeping the final specs and production dates under wraps, but the Tokyo concept gives us a solid hint at what they’re cooking up.

Rumor has it Honda might drop the HRC-K20C racing engine from the Super Taikyu Series into a street-legal Civic. That could mean more grunt than the standard FL5’s 315 horsepower, but Honda is still playing coy about whether the race-bred engine will actually make the cut.

Visually, the changes are impossible to miss. The concept gets a fresh front end, extra aero bits, flared and vented arches, chunkier side skirts, and a rear diffuser that means business. Top it off with a massive rear wing, Akrapovič exhaust, and HRC-branded cooling gear, and it’s clear this isn’t just a dress-up job.

Earlier reports indicated that HRC is also dialing in upgrades for the suspension, brakes, and cooling to match all that new aero. HRC boss Koji Watanabe has talked about bringing racing tech from Super Taikyu and Super GT into the mix, and it sounds like some aftermarket goodies will be part of the package too.

There’s still a lot up in the air – like just how much power this thing will make, or if Honda will sell it as a full-blown special edition or just offer a bunch of dealer-installed upgrades.

Honda

Honda Brings Its Racing Heritage to SEMA 2026

But the Civic Type R HRC Concept isn’t stealing the whole show. Honda’s SEMA booth will be packed with 12 race cars, from the wild new Passport Baja Spec Trophy Truck to a Hasport Prelude Club Racer, Felix Rosenqvist’s 2026 Indy 500-winning IndyCar, and the 2026 Acura ARX-06 GTP prototype.

The Passport Baja Spec is especially wild, packing a 2.4-liter twin-turbo V6 and an 800-volt hybrid setup for over 1,300 horsepower. Honda claims it’ll be the first hybrid Trophy Truck to take on the SCORE World Desert Championship.

HRC is also rolling out its first batch of Performance Parts for Honda and Acura street cars. We’re talking lightweight forged wheels, new exhausts, and a short-throw shifter for the Civic Type R, Integra Type S, and Passport TrailSport. After final testing, these goodies should hit dealerships and the Honda DreamShop in March 2027.

Porsche GT3 Driver Asks for Car Back After 127-MPH Police Chase

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

If you ever find yourself running from the cops in a high-performance German sports car, it is probably best not to ask for the vehicle back once you are finally in handcuffs. A 21-year-old driver in Everett, Washington, learned this lesson the hard way after leading police on a wild, high-speed pursuit. The driver, identified as Tyger S. Yong, was behind the wheel of a bright blue 2014 Porsche 911 GT3 when a Snohomish County sheriff’s deputy clocked him traveling at a blistering 127 mph near Interstate 5.

Rather than pulling over, the driver allegedly attempted to outrun law enforcement. While we frequently see stories regarding teens fleeing police in stolen muscle cars, doing so in a highly recognizable, track-focused Porsche is a uniquely terrible idea. The chase eventually came to an abrupt halt when deputies successfully deployed spike strips, destroying both of the GT3’s passenger-side tires and forcing the expensive sports car to a complete stop in traffic.

All Fun and Games Until It Gets Real

According to a report from Road & Track, bodycam footage released by the sheriff’s office captured the immediate aftermath of the arrest, providing a brutally honest exchange between the suspect and the arresting deputy. When confronted about why he continued speeding, Yong reportedly claimed he was simply trying to get home. The deputy immediately dismissed the excuse, stating that the suspect was clearly trying to escape.

The situation became even more surreal when Yong asked if there was anything he could do to get his car back. The deputy delivered a deadpan, reality-checking response: “Not run from the police,” before explicitly warning the young driver that his prized sports car would likely end up belonging to the sheriff’s office. To make matters worse, Yong’s wife was riding in the passenger seat during the 127 mph pursuit, leading deputies to refer a domestic-violence-designated reckless endangerment allegation due to the severe risk of injury.

Porsche

Will He Get It Back?

Watching a beautifully engineered 911 GT3 get impounded is always a painful sight for any enthusiast. While the manufacturer is currently relying on high-margin models to boost its profits and continues finding ways to make its highly allocated track cars even more exclusive, this specific GT3 is permanently off the consumer market.

The Snohomish County Sheriff’s Office currently has official possession of the 2014 Porsche and is actively pursuing its legal seizure under state laws regarding vehicles used in felony eluding. Ultimately, this high-speed stunt serves as an incredibly expensive reminder that attempting to outrun a police radio will almost always result in losing your license, your freedom, and your dream car.

Rivian's $59,485 R2 Outsold the R1S and R1T Combined in Q3

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

The R2

Rivian’s new midsize crossover performed well despite a high starting price. For now, the model starts at $59,485 for the R2 Performance with the Launch Package, but that hasn’t stopped customers from purchasing it anyway.

The rest of the lineup will follow in the near future. The Rivian R2 Performance, the sole trim since June this year, is the top-of-the-line variant, and it will eventually be joined by a mid-grade Premium and a base model that’ll only cost $46,485.

Rivian R2

Rivian

Outselling the R1S and R1T

Since June of this year, Rivian has noted that the demand for the R2 was pretty strong, and “reservation and conversion rates for the launch edition R2 remained higher in the quarter than our expectations,” said Rivian’s CEO, RJ Scaringe.

Now that the third quarter is finished, the company has since reported in its earnings call on October 29, 2026, that it outsold the bigger and more expensive R1S crossover and R1T pickup truck combined, becoming Rivian’s top-selling model in the quarter for the U.S. and Canada, while also beating out its sales for its electric delivery vans for Amazon.

The result can be attributed to how Rivian went about selling the R2. The brand raised its overall 2026 delivery guidance to between 67,000 and 70,000 vehicles. Previous delivery guidance was set between 65,000 and 70,000. Rivian also stated that it recently added a second shift at its Normal, Illinois, factory for the initial sales.

Rivian R2

Rivian

A Calculated Move

The R2 Performance launching first was no accident. Rivian stated that it’s “been able to very intentionally sequence the nature of launching with a high-price variant and then bringing in these other trims as our volume grows, such that in the end it supports a healthy, positive gross margin for the R2 business.”

High-end variants are usually where brands make the most money. With the R2 Performance, each sale returns a good margin back to Rivian for every car sold. When Rivian is ready to unleash its more affordable trims, it can potentially maintain the model’s sales momentum.

The R2 was well-received by the industry, and reviewers have praised it so far, along with a bit of controversy in tow. With 656 horsepower from a dual motor, all-wheel-drive, and a zero-to-sixty time of 3.6 seconds, it’s a rather exciting crossover. Couple that with a range of 330 miles, plus fast charging from 10% to 80% in under 30 minutes, and you have an EV that’s worthy of being a daily driver.

On top of that, the launch edition of the R2 comes with a lifetime Autonomy Plus driver-assistance subscription and a unique key fob in green, and an optional green exterior color and tow package.

Rivian R2

Rivian

Rivian Can’t Wait Too Long

With that being said, Rivian needs to strike while the iron is still hot and can’t wait too long to launch its more affordable trims. The R2 Performance starts at $59,485 and can go up from there.

Meanwhile, Rivian’s Premium trim, the mid-grade variant, will start at $55,485 (with shipping) and will come in “late 2026,” according to Rivian’s website. The midrange trim will come with 450 horsepower, all-wheel drive, and 330 miles of range. At the base model trim, the R2 Standard will start at $46,485 and come with 275 miles of range and be available as a rear-wheel-drive powertrain only. This model is expected to launch in summer 2027, according to Rivian.

Customers who want the base model will still be able to spec it out in case they want more capability. The long-range version will cost $3,500 more, but customers may also get long-range and all-wheel drive for $7,000.

McLaren Reveals Name and Power of Its Next Supercar as the 750S Faces the Axe

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Automotive  |  Read Source →

The Next Chapter Has a Name

McLaren has confirmed that a new supercar called the McL 820 will debut in 2027. Like many McLaren models, the number represents its power output, which, in this case, is 820 PS or 809 horsepower. What the press release didn’t confirm is whether the McL 820 will replace the brand’s flagship series-production model, the 750S.

However, that will likely be the case, which makes sense given that the latter is nearing the end of production and is taking final orders. The 750S is a heavily updated version of the 720S, which debuted in 2017 as the successor to the 650S. The Artura, on the other hand, isn’t expected to be discontinued until 2028. Still, with the British marque yet to confirm which model the new supercar will replace, nothing is set in stone.

McLaren

Building Its Own Muscle

Whatever the case may be, the McL 820 could benefit from McLaren’s plans to develop and manufacture engines and transmissions in-house under its expansion strategy. Historically, the automaker relied on British engineering company Ricardo to manufacture engines for its road cars, including the twin-turbo V8s used in the 750S lineage.

With McLaren developing new hybrid powertrains, the McL 820 could feature some form of electrification. Its 809 hp is up 69 hp over the 750S and 32 hp over the 788HS, a limited-edition model marking the end of the outgoing supercar’s production run.

McLaren has already shown its capabilities in the hybrid segment with the W1 hypercar, which combines a 4.0-liter twin-turbo V8 and an electric motor to produce 1,258 horsepower. Still, the 809-hp output puts the new supercar in the same ballpark as the Ferrari 296 GTB with its 819-hp hybrid V6, Ferrari 12Cilindri with its 819-hp NA V12, and Aston Martin Vanquish with its 824-hp twin-turbo V12.

McLaren

A Bigger Garage for McLaren

The McL 820 will be one of the new models joining the lineup this decade, alongside a potential 2+2 grand tourer and a confirmed SUV. The latter will let McLaren tap into the lucrative SUV segment, which has sustained demand, as seen with other supercar brands like Lamborghini and Aston Martin joining the fray with the Urus and DBX, respectively. What isn’t coming, at least anytime soon, is an all-electric model, something Ferrari has already embraced with the Luce.

In the U.S., McLaren is reportedly considering expanding its dealer network by up to 40% over the next two years amid growing demand stateside. The expansion could help the brand reach more buyers as it rolls out its next generation of supercars and enters new segments.

Winter Weather Advisory: Up to 10 Inches of Snow and 35 MPH Gusts Through Sunday

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Up to 10 inches of snow is forecast above 7,000 feet from Saturday evening into Sunday under a winter weather advisory from the National Weather Service, with higher totals possible above pass level and gusts as high as 35 mph. Forecasters expect slippery roads across the advisory area.

Snow Totals And Timing

The NWS Pocatello winter weather advisory runs from 8 p.m. ET Saturday to 7 p.m. ET Sunday for the Sun Valley region and the Big Lost Highlands and Copper Basin, including Galena Pass. Ketchum, Sun Valley, Galena and Copper Basin all fall inside it.

Snow is limited to elevations above 7,000 feet, where the office expects 4 to 10 inches, with more above pass level. Pocatello has already extended the advisory once.

State Highway 75 crosses Galena Summit north of Sun Valley, where snowfall is expected to increase above pass level. During one March storm, a slide buried the highway there under snow as deep as 20 feet in places, and Idaho Transportation Department crews worked 31 straight hours to clear it, KTVB reported.

Studded Tires Are Already Legal

Idaho’s studded tire season opened on Oct. 1. State law bans built-in studs on public highways only from May 1 to Sept. 30, so anyone who has already swapped to studs can run them through this storm. Tires with retractable studs are allowed year-round, as long as the studs retract to the wear bar when not in use, and they may only be deployed between Oct. 1 and April 30.

Chains are permitted at any time, and Idaho also accepts other traction devices if they grip at least as well as chains in the same conditions.

Cruise Control And Lane-Keeping On Icy Roads

ITD’s winter driving guidance is direct about driver aids. It tells drivers to avoid cruise control, lane-keeping and other auto-pilot features on wet or icy roads, alongside slowing down and leaving more stopping distance, and to brake gently and steer into a skid without overcorrecting.

The department also reminds drivers that bridges and overpasses freeze first and that the safest place near a plow is a comfortable distance behind it. Drivers can check current conditions through the Idaho 511 app or at 511.idaho.gov, a resource NWS Pocatello also recommends in the advisory.
