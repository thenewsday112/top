Winter Weather Advisory: Up to 10 Inches of Snow and 35 MPH Gusts Through Sunday

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Up to 10 inches of snow is forecast above 7,000 feet from Saturday evening into Sunday under a winter weather advisory from the National Weather Service, with higher totals possible above pass level and gusts as high as 35 mph. Forecasters expect slippery roads across the advisory area.

Snow Totals And Timing

The NWS Pocatello winter weather advisory runs from 8 p.m. ET Saturday to 7 p.m. ET Sunday for the Sun Valley region and the Big Lost Highlands and Copper Basin, including Galena Pass. Ketchum, Sun Valley, Galena and Copper Basin all fall inside it.

Snow is limited to elevations above 7,000 feet, where the office expects 4 to 10 inches, with more above pass level. Pocatello has already extended the advisory once.

State Highway 75 crosses Galena Summit north of Sun Valley, where snowfall is expected to increase above pass level. During one March storm, a slide buried the highway there under snow as deep as 20 feet in places, and Idaho Transportation Department crews worked 31 straight hours to clear it, KTVB reported.

Studded Tires Are Already Legal

Idaho’s studded tire season opened on Oct. 1. State law bans built-in studs on public highways only from May 1 to Sept. 30, so anyone who has already swapped to studs can run them through this storm. Tires with retractable studs are allowed year-round, as long as the studs retract to the wear bar when not in use, and they may only be deployed between Oct. 1 and April 30.

Chains are permitted at any time, and Idaho also accepts other traction devices if they grip at least as well as chains in the same conditions.

Cruise Control And Lane-Keeping On Icy Roads

ITD’s winter driving guidance is direct about driver aids. It tells drivers to avoid cruise control, lane-keeping and other auto-pilot features on wet or icy roads, alongside slowing down and leaving more stopping distance, and to brake gently and steer into a skid without overcorrecting.

The department also reminds drivers that bridges and overpasses freeze first and that the safest place near a plow is a comfortable distance behind it. Drivers can check current conditions through the Idaho 511 app or at 511.idaho.gov, a resource NWS Pocatello also recommends in the advisory.

Lamborghini Temerario Gets a Wild Two-Tone Makeover in Five Colors

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

A Two-Tone Treatment for the Baby Lambo

Lamborghini clearly wants the Temerario to stand out, and this time, it’s all about the paint job. Enter the Temerario Tettonero Capsule – a special edition that cranks up the two-tone look on this hybrid supercar.

Dreamed up by Lamborghini Centro Stile, this collection riffs on the capsule idea first seen on the Urus SE at Milan Design Week 2026. That one gave the Super SUV a taste of Tettonero, but now the same bold design lands on something much lower, sleeker, and a lot more focused on speed.

The main party trick here is the clash between your chosen paint and the deep Nero Noctis black accents. Pick from five wild colors: Giallo Inti, Arancio Xanto, Blu Cepheus, Verde Scandal, or Viola Pasifae. Giallo Inti is the launch star, but the rest let you dial in your own flavor.

Nero Noctis covers the hood, roof, and a bunch of custom exterior bits. The blacked-out look keeps going on the front splitter, rear diffuser, badge, and engine surround. Even the bumpers, mirrors, and wheels get the High Gloss Black treatment, just to make sure nobody misses the two-tone vibe.

Lamborghini

Details Continue Inside the Cabin

But the wild paint job is just the start. Lamborghini has snuck a few exclusive touches into the cabin too, even if they’re keeping most of them under wraps for now. The idea is to tie the whole look together without messing too much with the regular interior.

Inside, you get Nero Ade upholstery with Grigio Octans stitching for contrast. The Q-citura seats rock a hexagonal pattern, and you can even match the stitching to your chosen paint. It’s a neat way to link the outside and inside, especially if you’re the type who wants everything color-coordinated.

You also get embroidered Lamborghini logos on the headrests, Tettonero script on the doors, and a stitched two-tone outline of the rear end on the bulkhead. Each car gets a Matt Carbon plaque on the driver’s side, just to remind you it’s part of the special club.

Lamborghini

Same Electrified V8 Underneath

Naturally, all this flash doesn’t mess with what’s under the skin. The Temerario still packs the new 4.0-liter twin-turbo V8 and three electric motors beneath its custom suit.

That hybrid setup cranks out a combined 907 horsepower, and the V8 can scream all the way to 10,000 rpm. So you still get the wild performance you expect from a modern Lamborghini, just with a side of electrification.

Lamborghini hasn’t mentioned any mechanical tweaks for the Tettonero, so this one’s all about style points, not extra speed.

Toyota Unveils Five Rugged Woodland Concepts, Including A Lifted Sienna

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Toyota is coming to this year’s SEMA Show with something for everyone. Earlier this week, it unveiled the racy Crown Signia GT-S Concept, a much sportier take on the hybrid crossover for the street. The automaker has now taken the covers off something entirely different: A family of five Woodland concepts. Unlike the Crown Signia GT-S, the Woodland concepts reimagine a lineup of rugged, more capable Toyotas that can tackle a dirt road or campsite trail as effortlessly as the daily commute.

Five Concepts, One Family Look

Toyota Woodland Concepts

Toyota

Toyota chose the following five models as the basis for the Woodland concepts:

  • Toyota RAV4
  • Toyota Grand Highlander
  • Toyota Sienna
  • Toyota bZ
  • Toyota Crown Signia

Toyota RAV4 Woodland Concept

Toyota

View the 5 images of this gallery on the original article

All models are finished in striking Horizon Blue paint with contrasting black hoods/roofs. The blue shade was used on classic Toyota models in the 1960s, including the Stout, Crown, and Corona. CALTY Design Research created a visual and functional identity for all five concepts, which are said to be built around exploration without compromising usability. They all feature lift kits, satin-black wheels wrapped in all-terrain tires, roof racks, cross bars, and aggressive over-fenders.

My pick for the coolest, most unexpected model in the quintet has to be the Sienna Woodland. It couldn’t be further removed from the conservative hybrid minivan that is more often seen at the mall or offloading kids at schools across the country. While most Toyota SUVs have rugged, off-road trims, the Sienna does not, but what could be better than an eight-seater van that can handle some mild off-roading?

Rugged Performance, Regardless of The Powertrain

Toyota Grand Highlander Woodland Concept

Toyota

View the 3 images of this gallery on the original article

With the Woodland concepts, Toyota also aims to show that capability need not be limited to one powertrain type. The models here include hybrid, plug-in hybrid, and battery-electric options. 

While no specifications were shared, we can get a sense of what’s possible from the production Woodland models Toyota already sells. For instance, the bZ Woodland electric SUV has standard all-wheel drive and a handy 8.4 inches of ground clearance. It also features X-Mode, which optimizes power delivery and brake control, improving traction across loose surfaces. Another X-Mode function is Grip Control, a system that modulates power delivery in tough conditions, while reacting to different surface conditions. The RAV4 Woodland also has more ground clearance than standard trims and comes with extras like all-terrain tires and a tow hitch.

Toyota Will Thrill Off-Road Fans At SEMA

Toyota RAV4 Woodland Concept

Toyota

View the 3 images of this gallery on the original article

All in all, it’s shaping up to be an exciting SEMA Show for Toyota fans, especially those into off-roading. Besides the Crown Signia GTS and Woodland concepts, the automaker already showed off Tacoma and Tundra pickups converted into snow machines—probably the wildest creations yet from the Japanese brand. While those snow machines are unlikely to influence production models, the new Woodland concepts are much likelier to inspire a Toyota that could end up on the showroom floor. With Honda dialing up the intensity for its TrailSport lineup, Toyota could do the same with the Woodland range.

These concepts and other models will be showcased at the event from November 3, 2026, with public access from November 6. The event takes place at the Las Vegas Convention Center.
